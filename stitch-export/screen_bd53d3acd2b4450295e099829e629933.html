04 // EXECUTIVE CODE OF CRAFT

Leadership Principles Formed in Production

Systems architecture is not drawing lines in diagramming tools—it is aligning organization incentives, managing cognitive load, and prioritizing business durability over developer novelty.

“Any architecture you cannot explain to both a board member and a newly joined junior engineer in three minutes is a design failure.”

01

Code is a Liability; Architecture is Leverage

Every line of written code requires patching, testing, and cognitive overhead. The highest-leverage engineering decisions often delete 40,000 lines of home-grown abstractions in favor of battle-tested primitives.

02

Boring Technology for Critical Paths, Differentiators for Edge

Store ledger state and critical money movement in PostgreSQL, Raft, and Linux primitives. Spend your innovation tokens strictly where your business creates 10x competitive product value.

03

High Psychological Safety, Uncompromising Craft

Engineers who fear making mistakes will hide architectural flaws until they explode in a production incident. True leadership creates blameless curiosity paired with obsessive operational rigour.
